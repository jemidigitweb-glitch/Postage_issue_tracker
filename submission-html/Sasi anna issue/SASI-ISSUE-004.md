# System Stock Discrepancies and Inactive Listings

## Issue ID

---

## Issue Summary

There is a mismatch between physical inventory and the digital system (Kronos/eBay), where items physically in stock are shown as inactive ("off") or displaying negative balances.

---

## Current Operational Process

Staff review system reports for "minus" stock and attempt to reconcile them with physical counts in the store. Listings on platforms like eBay are manually checked to see if they are active or inactive.

---

## Business Problem

Potential sales are lost because available products (specifically transformers) are not visible to customers, and inaccurate data makes inventory management difficult.

---

## Root Cause

### Confirmed Facts

Items like small transformers (Blue, Orange, Black & White) are physically in the warehouse but show as inactive or negative in the system.

### Assumptions

It is assumed that listings were accidentally turned off or that the system failed to update when 500 units were received from Kronos.

---

## Business Impact

Reduced sales volume for "good moving" items and wasted labor hours spent on manual reconciliation.

---

## Operational Risks

Overselling (if negative stock is incorrect) or missed revenue (if active stock is hidden).

---

## Existing Workaround

A manual "checking" process where a person physically verifies the store stock and then updates the platform status.

---

## Recommended Next Actions

Conduct a full physical audit of the "minus" stock items and sync the "Kronos" intake (500 units) immediately into the active system.
