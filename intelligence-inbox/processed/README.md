# intelligence-inbox/processed/

## Why this folder exists

This folder is the archive for intelligence-inbox entries that have been reviewed,
actioned, or closed. It preserves the history of issues and gaps without cluttering
the active intake folders.

## What belongs here

Entries moved from daily-issues/ or document-gaps/ once they are:
- Fully resolved with a confirmed outcome
- Reviewed and closed without requiring action
- Superseded by a newer entry or decision

Keep the original filename. Add a note at the top of the file with the resolution
date and outcome summary before archiving.

## What does NOT belong here

- Active issues still being tracked — keep those in daily-issues/
- Open gaps awaiting Varmen confirmation — keep those in document-gaps/
- New content of any kind — this folder receives moved files only
