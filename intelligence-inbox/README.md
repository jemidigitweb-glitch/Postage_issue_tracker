# intelligence-inbox/

## Why this folder exists

This folder is the live intake point for operational knowledge that enters the
AIOS after the initial build. It is the engine of the intelligence loop — the
mechanism by which daily operational experience improves the system over time.

## What belongs here

Content is routed into three subfolders:
- daily-issues/ — structured log entries for postage issues raised each day
- document-gaps/ — gaps identified in Laksika's source documents, flagged for Varmen
- processed/ — entries that have been reviewed and acted on, archived here

## What does NOT belong here

- Permanent reference content — that belongs in context/
- Skill definitions — those belong in skills/
- Confirmed decisions or gap resolutions — those belong in decisions/
- Raw unstructured notes without a date, type, and owner
