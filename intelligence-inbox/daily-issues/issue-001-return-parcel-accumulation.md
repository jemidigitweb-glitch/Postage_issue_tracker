## Confirmed Facts

The following facts are confirmed from the warehouse voice recording, supporting Royal
Mail shipping label image, and NotebookLM extraction. No facts have been inferred
or added beyond what the source materials confirm.

- Customers return parcels they no longer want.
- Returned parcels are sent to the documented return address.
- Approximately three pallets of returned parcels are currently accumulated.
- Ownership and handling responsibility for returned parcels are not yet confirmed.
- Future expensive parcels may also accumulate if the return handling process
  remains unclear or undocumented.

---

## Evidence

**Source:** Warehouse voice recording and supporting Royal Mail shipping label image,
extracted and summarised via NotebookLM.

**Summary of NotebookLM findings:**

The recording and image confirm that returned parcels are physically present in the
warehouse in significant volume — approximately three pallets. The return address
on the Royal Mail label is consistent with the documented return address used in
Postage operations. The recording indicates that there is no clear, documented
process currently governing what happens to parcels once they arrive back at the
warehouse after a return. The recording raises concern that as the volume of
returned parcels grows — particularly if higher-value items are involved — the
absence of a defined handling workflow becomes an operational risk.

No confirmation was provided in the recording regarding who owns the parcels once
returned, who is responsible for processing them, or what escalation path exists
if the volume or value of accumulation becomes a problem.

## Unknowns

The following items are explicitly unknown at the time of this record. They are
recorded here as investigation targets — not as gaps in confirmed fact.

- Who owns the returned parcels once they arrive at the warehouse?
- Who manages the return handling process?
- What is the current handling workflow (if any) for returned parcels?
- What controls currently exist over returned parcel storage and processing?
- What happens after returned parcels arrive — are they inspected, restocked,
  disposed of, or held pending a decision?
