# Issue 011 – Assembly Hardware Mismatch for Small Diamond Cage (WC SLBM)

**Date Logged:** 2026-07-07
**Logged By:** Vishnusri
**Status:** Open — Under Investigation
**Classification:** Daily Issue

---

## Issue Summary

The small diamond cage (Part No: WC SLBM) is currently listed and assembled using a half-thin
shelf holder. The internal clearance of the WC SLBM cage is insufficient to allow the securing
ring to be manually tightened when a half-thin fitting is used, making assembly physically
difficult and inefficient.

The operational team has identified that a full-thin shelf holder together with a 2-metre cable
assembly resolves the clearance problem and allows the product to be assembled correctly. However,
the product listing and the back list have not yet been updated to reflect this configuration.
The listing continues to specify the half-thin hardware, which does not match the assembly
configuration currently in use.

---

## Business Problem

The core problem is a mismatch between the hardware specified in the product listing and the
hardware that the WC SLBM cage physically requires for assembly:

- **The product listing specifies a half-thin shelf holder.** This is the configuration currently
  recorded in both the product listing and the back list for WC SLBM units.
- **The WC SLBM cage has insufficient internal clearance for half-thin fittings.** The narrow gap
  inside the cage prevents the securing ring from being tightened manually when a half-thin fitting
  is present, making assembly slow, difficult, and inconsistent.
- **The operational team is assembling the product using a different configuration.** A full-thin
  shelf holder together with a 2-metre cable is being used in practice because this configuration
  fits the cage correctly. This works operationally but the listing does not reflect it.
- **The product listing and the physical assembly are inconsistent.** Any team member, warehouse
  operative, or external party who follows the product listing will attempt to assemble the cage
  with half-thin hardware — producing the same assembly difficulty every time.
- **The back list has also not been updated.** The back list continues to specify half-thin hardware,
  meaning the error is present in more than one operational document.

---

## Current Operational Process

The current assembly and listing process for the small diamond cage (WC SLBM) is as follows:

1. **Product is listed with half-thin hardware specification** — the product listing for WC SLBM
   units currently specifies a half-thin shelf holder as the required assembly component. This
   specification is also reflected in the back list for these units.
2. **Assembly is attempted using half-thin fittings** — warehouse or assembly staff following the
   listing would select a half-thin shelf holder and attempt to complete the assembly.
3. **Assembly difficulty is encountered** — the internal clearance of the WC SLBM small diamond
   cage is too narrow for the ring to be tightened manually onto the half-thin fitting. The
   restricted space makes assembly physically difficult and time-consuming.
4. **Alternative configuration is used in practice** — the operational team has adopted the use
   of a full-thin shelf holder together with a 2-metre cable assembly, which provides the
   clearance needed to complete the assembly correctly.
5. **Listing and back list remain unchanged** — despite the alternative configuration being used
   in practice, neither the product listing nor the back list has been updated to specify the
   correct hardware.

**Failure point:** Steps 4 and 5 are in conflict. The assembly approach being used in practice
differs from the assembly approach specified in the product documentation. Any staff member
following the listing will encounter the same assembly problem, and the correct configuration
will not be consistently applied unless the documentation is corrected.

## Root Cause

**Not Yet Confirmed.**

The available evidence confirms that the half-thin hardware configuration is physically
incompatible with the WC SLBM small diamond cage, and that the product listing continues
to specify this incompatible configuration. However, the reason why the incorrect hardware
specification remains in the product listing and back list has not been confirmed and
requires investigation.

The following possibilities exist but none have been confirmed:

- The hardware incompatibility may have been identified after the listing was first created,
  and the listing was never subsequently corrected
- The correct configuration may have been known internally but not communicated to the team
  responsible for maintaining the listing and back list
- A documentation oversight may have resulted in the back list being carried forward without
  review when the assembly approach changed

No assumption in this list should be treated as fact. Investigation is required before any
corrective action is finalised.

## Existing Workaround

The operational team is currently assembling WC SLBM units using a full-thin shelf holder
together with a 2-metre cable assembly. This configuration provides sufficient internal
clearance inside the small diamond cage to allow the securing ring to be tightened correctly.

This is the current operational practice adopted by the reporting team. It is not a formally
approved or documented workaround — the product listing and back list continue to specify the
half-thin configuration, and the revised approach has not yet been validated through a formal
physical assembly test.

Until the listing and back list are corrected and the revised configuration is formally
validated, this workaround depends on individual staff knowledge of the correct approach
rather than documented guidance.

---

## Recommended Next Actions

1. **Update the product listing for WC SLBM** — change the hardware specification from
   half-thin shelf holder to full-thin shelf holder with 2-metre cable assembly to accurately
   reflect the configuration required for correct assembly.
2. **Update the back list accordingly** — correct the back list to specify the full-thin
   shelf holder and remove the half-thin specification from all WC SLBM entries.
3. **Remove the half-thin specification from all WC SLBM product documentation** — confirm
   that no other documents, instructions, or procurement records continue to reference the
   half-thin configuration for this product.
4. **Validate the revised configuration through a physical assembly test** — before finalising
   the updated listing, confirm that the full-thin shelf holder with 2-metre cable resolves the
   clearance problem and allows the product to be assembled correctly under normal conditions.
5. **Confirm the root cause** — investigate why the incompatible half-thin specification remained
   in the listing and back list, to determine whether a process or documentation control is needed
   to prevent similar mismatches from being introduced in future product configurations.
