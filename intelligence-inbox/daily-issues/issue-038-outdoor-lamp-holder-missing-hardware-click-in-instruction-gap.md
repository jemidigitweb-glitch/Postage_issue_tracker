## Issue Summary

A customer (Derek Fraser) reported "parts missing" for an order of the
IP44 Rainproof Outdoor E27 Lamp Holder (2-pack), SKU LHXSHE27BM2PK.

The customer stated that both holders arrived without internal screws and
requested replacements including the screws.

Internal communication indicates that the product uses a click-in assembly
mechanism rather than screws. Because neither the assembly instructions nor
the pack list for LHXSHE27BM2PK explain this click-in mechanism, the customer
had no documented basis for understanding how the product is assembled. The
customer assumed the absence of screws represented a packing error.

The reported order value is £14.89.

---

## Business Problem

- **Customer expectation versus product design.** The customer expected screws
  for assembly. Internal communication indicates the product uses a click-in
  mechanism instead. The gap between these two states was caused by absent
  documentation, not a packing error.
- **Unnecessary replacement activity.** Replacement requests for screws that
  are not part of the product design waste inventory, staff time, and shipping
  cost.
- **Repetition risk.** Without a documentation update, future sales of
  LHXSHE27BM2PK are likely to generate the same class of missing-hardware
  claim.
- **Customer satisfaction risk.** A customer who cannot assemble the product
  due to absent instructions may leave negative reviews or return the item.

## Root Cause

**Confirmed:** The assembly instructions for SKU LHXSHE27BM2PK do not
currently explain the click-in assembly mechanism.

**Confirmed:** The pack list does not adequately detail the components or
assembly method for this product.

These two absences are the direct cause of the customer's missing-hardware
claim. A customer who understands that the product uses click-in assembly
would not expect screws to be included.

## Fix and Action Required

1. **Create click-in assembly instructions.** Prepare a clear guide explaining
   the click-in assembly mechanism for the top part of the LHXSHE27BM2PK
   lamp holder. The guide must make explicit that screws are not required and
   that the fitting is achieved by the click-in design.

2. **Update the pack list.** Revise the pack list for SKU LHXSHE27BM2PK to
   include the click-in assembly information and to correctly describe the
   expected components so customers have the information needed to assemble
   the product.

3. **Audit the product listing.** Check whether the online product description
   incorrectly mentions or implies that screws are required or expected. This
   is a mandatory audit action — do NOT assume the listing is incorrect until
   the audit is completed and its finding is confirmed.

4. **Resolve Derek Fraser's request.** Confirm whether a replacement was
   appropriate and, if so, whether the customer has been supported. If
   no replacement was dispatched, consider whether providing an assembly
   explanation resolves the complaint.

5. **Verify mechanism scope.** Confirm whether other SKUs in the lamp holder
   range use the same click-in mechanism. If they do, those SKUs require the
   same pack-list and instruction update.
