## Issue Summary

DPD has failed to provide the requested high-capacity collection vehicle
(large van) for scheduled parcel pickups, despite prior notification that
such a vehicle would be required for the duration of the current week.
This has resulted in a parcel collection backlog: the vehicle DPD sent
was insufficient to transport the daily parcel volume in a single trip.

the vendor site. No formal workaround is currently in place.

A management decision is required on whether to continue with DPD under a
satisfactory capacity arrangement or to evaluate an alternative logistics
provider capable of meeting the required collection volume.
