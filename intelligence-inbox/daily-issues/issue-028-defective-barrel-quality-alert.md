## Issue Summary

SKU WCB6BC (barrel — metal lampshade) has been received from the supplier in a
defective state. Units have arrived bent. The defect is physical — the barrel
component is deformed on receipt.

The cause of the deformation has not been confirmed. It is not yet established
whether the defect originates from the supplier's manufacturing process, from
transit damage, or from a packaging failure. No inbound inspection process or
supplier defect escalation procedure is documented in the AIOS at time of logging.

  Do not return to general stock.

- Raise the defect with the supplier immediately. Provide photographic evidence

  packaging, or handling.

- Determine disposition of defective stock: rework, return to supplier, or
  write-off.

**Long term:**

  for a Document Gap assessment.
