## Issue Summary

Two boxes of Pendant Light Fixture (Full Set) have been observed on the same warehouse shelf, one
labelled LEDSone and one labelled DC Voltage. A warehouse team member photographed both boxes and
annotated the image with the question "Same?" — indicating uncertainty about whether these are the
same product under two different brands.

A separate photograph shows two internal lamp sockets from the Pendant Light Fixture range placed
side by side: one with a black interior and one with a white interior. The black-interior socket is
circled and the white-interior socket is circled and marked with an X, with a "?" annotation below —
indicating that the socket colour difference has been noticed and its significance is unresolved.

Both observations were recorded on 2026-07-09 and are supported by warehouse photographic evidence.

## Operational Risk

The following risks arise from the confirmed observations, pending investigation:

| Risk | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Incorrect fulfilment | If LEDSone-branded and DC Voltage-branded boxes are picked interchangeably without knowing whether they are the same product, customers may receive a product under a different brand name than the one listed. |
| Return and restocking confusion | If both branded variants are shelved together and treated as the same stock, returns of one brand may be restocked and re-dispatched as the other brand, leading to an uncontrolled mix. |
| Black vs white socket dispatch inconsistency | If the black-interior and white-interior socket variants are not the same specification, dispatching either variant interchangeably could result in customers receiving a product that does not match the listing. |
| Inventory record inaccuracy | If two distinct products share a shelf location or a single SKU without clear differentiation, inventory counts for the Pendant Light Fixture range may not reflect actual stock by variant. |
| No documented identification process | There is currently no confirmed written process for identifying which branded box or which socket variant to pick for a given order. |

These are risks arising from the confirmed observations. They are NOT confirmed as having caused
fulfilment failures, returns, or customer complaints. This distinction must be preserved.

---

## Business Impact

Business impact is stated as a risk because no customer outcome has been confirmed:

- If the two branded products are different, dispatching the wrong brand fulfils an order incorrectly
  and may result in customer dissatisfaction or a return.
- If the socket variants differ in specification, customers may receive a product that does not match
  what they ordered or what the listing describes.
- The absence of a documented identification rule means staff currently have no authoritative guide
  to follow when both branded variants are present on the same shelf.

## Recommended Next Actions

The following actions are recommended pending reviewer approval. They are not approved operational
rules and must not be treated as confirmed procedures until confirmed by Varmen or Laksika.

1. **Physical product identification**: A warehouse team member should inspect both branded boxes
   and confirm whether the contents are physically identical or different.
2. **SKU verification**: Check the LEDSone OMS to confirm whether both branded variants are mapped
   to the same SKU or to separate SKUs.
3. **Socket specification check**: Confirm whether the black-interior and white-interior sockets
   are the same specification (material, electrical rating, E27 thread standard) or different.
4. **Picking rule decision**: Once the above is confirmed, a business decision should establish
   whether the two branded variants may be picked interchangeably or must be segregated.
5. **Shelf location review**: Confirm whether both branded variants share a shelf location and
   whether a change is required.
6. **Order and return review**: Review recent orders and returns for the Pendant Light Fixture range
   to determine whether any fulfilment errors or return reasons can be attributed to brand confusion.
