## Issue Summary

A minimum selling price policy of €8.00 has been set for German marketplace listings. Any listing priced below €8.00 must either add a €4.99 shipping charge or be converted into a 2-pack listing to meet this minimum.

---

Not Available in the Evidence — the current listing/pricing setup process on the German marketplace prior to this policy is not described in detail.

---

## Business Problem

Some German marketplace listings may currently be priced below €8.00 without either of the two accepted remedies (added shipping charge or 2-pack conversion) applied, making them non-compliant with the new minimum price policy.

---

## Root Cause

**Status: Not Yet Confirmed**

### Confirmed Facts

The German marketplace minimum price policy is set at €8.00. Two accepted compliance paths exist for listings below that threshold: (a) add a €4.99 shipping charge, or (b) convert the listing into a 2-pack.

### Assumptions

Not Available in the Evidence.

## Business Impact

Listings that remain priced below €8.00 without applying either remedy are non-compliant with the policy and may misrepresent true per-unit economics on the German marketplace.

---

## Operational Risks

Continued sale of non-compliant sub-€8.00 listings if the existing catalog is not audited; inconsistent application of the policy to newly created listings if the rule is not enforced at publish time.

---

## Existing Workaround

Not Available in the Evidence.

---

## Recommended Next Actions

Ensure no German marketplace listings are priced below €8.00. For any listing priced below €8.00, either add a €4.99 shipping charge or convert it into a 2-pack. Review all existing German marketplace listings and update any that do not comply with the €8.00 minimum price policy. Going forward, do not create or publish any German marketplace listing priced below €8.00 unless the €4.99 shipping fee has been applied or the product has been converted to a 2-pack listing.
