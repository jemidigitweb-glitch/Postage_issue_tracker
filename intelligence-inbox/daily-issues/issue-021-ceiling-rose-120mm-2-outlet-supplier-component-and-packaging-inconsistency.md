## Executive Summary

Warehouse staff are reportedly performing manual piece-by-piece accessory checks on individual
units of the 120mm 2-Outlet Black Ceiling Rose (CRSF1202BM) before dispatch. The supplied
Business Intelligence Report states that different suppliers provide varying cord grip types
for this product, and that small accessory items may be packed loosely or in insufficiently
sealed internal sub-packaging, creating a risk that parts fall out.

The evidence image (`2026_07_12_p1.png`) confirms the product identity (SKU CRSF1202BM,
120mm, 2 Outlet, Black) but does not show cord grip variation, loose accessories, or
packaging condition. All claims about supplier component inconsistency and packaging
inadequacy are drawn from the supplied BI report and audio recordings that have not been
independently transcribed.

No confirmed customer complaints, returns, or financial losses have been established from
available evidence. All reported business impacts are potential risks pending investigation.

The absence of a validated supplier specification for cord grip type and sealed accessory
packaging is currently an unproven assumption, not a confirmed fact. A Document Gap is not
created at this stage because the structural control absence has not been independently
proven from repository evidence.

## Issue Summary

Warehouse staff are reportedly required to manually inspect individual units of CRSF1202BM
to confirm that accessory components are complete before dispatch. The reported root cause

during warehouse handling. Whether this check is applied consistently, how it is tracked,

## Business Problem

**Reported — potential impacts, not confirmed incidents:**

The reported business problem has two components:

1. **Supplier component variation** — CRSF1202BM units from different suppliers reportedly
   contain different cord grip types. If this is confirmed, it creates visual inconsistency
   in the delivered product and potential ambiguity at the warehouse picking and packing stage
   about which cord grip type is correct for the SKU.

2. **Insecure accessory packaging** — Small accessories within the product packaging are
   reportedly not always securely sealed in internal sub-packaging. If accessories fall out
   before dispatch, units may be incomplete when received by the customer.

The combination of these two reported problems may require warehouse staff to spend
additional time on individual unit inspection that would not be necessary if component
standardization and sealed packaging were confirmed and enforced with suppliers.
