## Issue Summary

When Sales follows the exact prices provided by INV/Finance and those prices are later found to be uncompetitive or unprofitable, the resulting sales decline is being attributed to the Sales team's performance rather than to the pricing input itself.

---

## Current Operational Process

Sales executes against whatever validated pricing and cost data management provides. Sales has full visibility into external, platform-side data (platform-level cost data, ads data, competitor pricing, search ranking position, Amazon's and eBay's own validated ACOS benchmarks, buyer-facing market movement) but no visibility into internal cost data — Finance's cost calculations, postage/packaging cost allocation, and how "net sales" is derived internally.

---

## Business Problem

Sales is being judged on outcomes driven by pricing inputs it did not set and could not see in advance. There is no jointly agreed ACOS/profitability threshold defined at the point pricing decisions are made — "acceptable" is only defined after the fact, once Finance's review is delivered, up to 6 months later.

---

## Root Cause

**Status: Not Yet Confirmed**

### Confirmed Facts

Sales is fully accountable for sales numbers and executes against whatever validated pricing and cost data management provides. What Sales cannot do is absorb accountability for outcomes driven by inputs it did not set, could not see in advance, and — in this case — requested three months ago and still has not received (see issue-073).

### Assumptions

Not Available in the Evidence.

---

## Business Impact

Sales performance reviews and internal narrative may incorrectly attribute pricing-driven sales decline to Sales execution rather than to the pricing/cost inputs supplied by INV and Finance, which Sales had no visibility into at the time of pricing.

---

## Operational Risks

Continued misattribution erodes trust between Sales, Finance, and Postage/Packing, and removes the incentive for Sales to flag pricing risk early if it will be blamed for outcomes regardless.

---

## Existing Workaround

Not Available in the Evidence.

---

## Recommended Next Actions

Establish a jointly agreed ACOS/profitability threshold that both Sales and Finance operate from, with no ambiguity about what "acceptable" looks like at the point pricing decisions are made — not 6 months later. This should be agreed and signed off jointly by Finance, Postage/Packing, and Sales team leaders as part of the same working session covering the Net Sales Calculation Standard (issue-070).
