# Issue 010 – Missing Stock: Twisted and Hook Variants of E27 Lamp Holder Not Received

**Date Logged:** 2026-07-07
**Logged By:** Vishnusri
**Status:** Open — Under Investigation
**Classification:** Daily Issue

---

## Issue Summary

The twisted variant of the E27 Vintage Industrial Lamp Light Bulb Holder has not been received
at the warehouse, despite the round/copper variant (SKU LHNHE27YB) being confirmed as in stock
and currently available for dispatch. A related variant described as the hook version has also
not been received. Both missing variants are recorded as historically fast-selling items.

The absence of these variants prevents the business from fulfilling orders for these specific
product types, while orders for the round/copper variant continue to be processed normally.
The cause of the missing stock has not yet been confirmed and requires investigation.

---

## Business Problem

The core problem is a supply gap for two high-demand product variants within the E27 Vintage
Industrial Lamp Light Bulb Holder range:

- **The twisted variant has not arrived at the warehouse.** Because this variant has historically
  sold quickly, its absence directly prevents the business from fulfilling customer demand for
  this specific product type.
- **The hook variant has also not been received.** Its procurement history requires review to
  determine when it was last received and whether an expected delivery is outstanding.
- **The round/copper variant (SKU LHNHE27YB) is confirmed in stock** and continues to be
  dispatched. However, the availability of one variant does not resolve the supply gap for the
  other two variants, which have distinct product characteristics.
- **The absence of high-demand variants results in missed revenue opportunities** for product
  lines that have demonstrated strong historical sales performance.
- **Without a confirmed cause for the missing stock**, the business cannot determine whether the
  shortage is temporary (shipping delay in transit) or requires escalation to the procurement team.

---

## Current Operational Process

The current warehouse fulfilment workflow for fast-selling inventory items in this product range
is as follows:

1. **Stock monitoring across warehouse units** — stock levels for items in this product range
   are tracked across multiple UK warehouse units, including UK Unit 18, UK Unit 3, and UK Unit 4.
   Specific storage locations such as Location 1-A-02 A are used to identify where items are held.
2. **Order picking against available stock** — warehouse staff pick items against incoming orders
   based on available stock records. For the E27 Vintage Industrial Lamp Light Bulb Holder product
   range, the round/copper variant (SKU LHNHE27YB) is currently being picked and dispatched
   normally from UK Unit 3.
3. **Variant-level stock tracking** — the inventory system records stock levels per variant and
   per unit. SKU PSOS2HETBM currently shows 33 units available in UK Unit 3 and zero units in
   both UK Unit 18 and UK Unit 4.
4. **Dispatch via packlist** — dispatched items are recorded in daily packlists stored in Dropbox.
   The warehouse evidence confirms the round/copper variant was included in a 2nd Booking packlist
   dated 03 July 2026.

**Failure point:** The twisted and hook variants of the E27 lamp holder are not present in the
warehouse as available stock. The absence was identified during stock monitoring. No automated
check currently exists to flag when one variant of a fast-selling product range is received
without its associated variants.

## Root Cause

**Not Yet Confirmed.**

The approved BI report records three possible causes as assumptions only — none have been
confirmed by investigation:

- A procurement error may have resulted in the twisted variant not being ordered
- A shipping delay from the supplier may mean the item is in transit but has not yet arrived
- A warehouse intake oversight may mean the item was received but not recorded or placed correctly

None of these assumptions should be treated as confirmed fact. The investigation defined in
Recommended Next Actions must be completed before any determination is made.

## Existing Workaround

A partial workaround is currently in operation.

The round/copper variant of the E27 Vintage Industrial Lamp Light Bulb Holder (SKU LHNHE27YB)
is confirmed in stock and is being dispatched against orders. The warehouse evidence (packlist
dated 03.07.2026) confirms this variant is being picked and dispatched normally from UK Unit 3.

This workaround addresses demand for the round/copper variant only. It does not fulfil demand
for the twisted or hook variants, which have distinct product characteristics from the round
version. Customers ordering the twisted or hook variant are not served by the availability
of the round/copper variant.

No confirmed workaround currently exists for the missing twisted and hook variants specifically.

## Recommended Next Actions

1. **Review shipping manifests** — check incoming delivery records to determine whether the
   twisted variant was dispatched by the supplier. Confirm whether an expected delivery is
   outstanding or whether no order was placed.
2. **Conduct a physical cycle count** — perform a physical count at Location 1-A-02 A and
   surrounding storage areas in UK Unit 3, UK Unit 4, and UK Unit 18 to confirm the twisted
   and hook variants are not physically present but misplaced or mis-scanned on arrival.
3. **Review procurement history for the hook variant** — confirm the last recorded receipt
   of the hook variant and determine whether a replenishment order has been placed.
4. **Clarify SKU PSOS2HETBM variant association** — confirm which product variant SKU PSOS2HETBM
   refers to and whether the zero stock in UK Unit 18 and UK Unit 4 is part of the same supply
   gap described in this issue.
5. **Distribute stock across units once variants are located or received** — once the missing
   variants are confirmed as received or located, ensure stock is distributed appropriately
   across UK Unit 18, UK Unit 3, and UK Unit 4 to support fulfilment from all units.
6. **Escalate to procurement if stock is not located** — if the cycle count confirms no physical
   stock exists and no delivery is in transit, escalate to the procurement team immediately to
   initiate a reorder for the twisted and hook variants.
