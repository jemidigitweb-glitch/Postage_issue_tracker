## Issue Summary

Selling "HomeIn" (German/European) stock through different company accounts requires a complex manual process of "buying and selling" between internal accounts to maintain balance.

---

## Current Operational Process

When stock from the HomeIn account (Italy items/German system) is sold via another account, staff must manually record it as a sale from one and a purchase by the other to "convert" the transaction.

---

## Business Problem

The process is administratively heavy and prone to accounting errors because the systems (UK vs. German/European) do not talk to each other.

---

## Root Cause

**Status: Not Yet Confirmed**

### Confirmed Facts

HomeIn uses a German system/platform while other stock is UK-based.

### Assumptions

There is currently no automated software bridge to handle inter-company stock transfers or cross-account sales.

---

## Business Impact

Increased administrative overhead and complex financial reporting.

---

## Operational Risks

Tax or audit discrepancies if the manual "buying and selling" between accounts is not recorded accurately.

---

## Existing Workaround

Manually "buying" the stock back and forth between accounts to ensure the listings and finances match.

---

## Recommended Next Actions

Develop or implement a "system to convert" or synchronize these accounts to automate the inter-company transfer records.

---

## AIOS Classification

Decision Required.

Management needs to decide whether to invest in an automated cross-platform accounting system or continue with the manual workarounds.

---
