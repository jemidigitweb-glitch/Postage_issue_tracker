## Gap Summary

No assembly instructions currently explain the click-in mechanism for the
IP44 Rainproof Outdoor E27 Lamp Holder (2-pack), SKU LHXSHE27BM2PK.

The current pack list for this SKU does not adequately describe the
components or the click-in assembly method.

As a result, customers who expect a conventional screw-assembly product
have no documented basis for understanding how the lamp holder is actually
assembled. This absence caused a customer (ISSUE-038) to report "parts
missing" and request screw replacements for a product that reportedly
uses click-in assembly and does not include or require screws.

The gap is not a packing error. It is a documentation content gap: the
product's assembly mechanism has not been communicated to the customer through
the pack list or assembly instructions.

## Missing Documentation

### 1. Click-In Assembly Instructions for LHXSHE27BM2PK

A clear, customer-facing guide explaining how the top part of the
IP44 Rainproof Outdoor E27 Lamp Holder (LHXSHE27BM2PK) is assembled using
the click-in mechanism.

The guide must communicate:

- That the product is assembled using a click-in method.
- That screws are not required and are not a missing component.
- The physical steps needed to complete the click-in assembly (to be
  determined by the product team with reference to the actual product).

**Currently missing:** No such customer-facing assembly guide exists for
this SKU or is included in the pack list or product materials.

### 2. Adequate Pack-List Content for LHXSHE27BM2PK

A revised pack list that correctly describes:

- The components included in the 2-pack.
- The assembly method (click-in).
- An explicit statement that screws are not part of the product design.

**Currently missing:** The current pack list does not adequately describe
the components or the assembly method, creating a structural expectation
gap for the customer.

### 3. Product Listing Accuracy Confirmation (Audit Required)

A verified check of the online product listing for LHXSHE27BM2PK to
determine whether the listing incorrectly mentions or implies that screws
are required for assembly.

**Status:** Unconfirmed — a product listing audit is required before any
finding can be stated. Do not assume the listing is incorrect until the
audit is complete and its result is recorded.

## Distinctness from GAP-006

GAP-006 (Assembly Instruction Technical Sign-Off and Product-to-Manual
Verification) identifies the absence of an internal process to verify
instruction accuracy before supplier mass printing. It is a **process gap**.

GAP-008 identifies the absence of **content**: the click-in mechanism is
not explained in any customer-facing documentation for LHXSHE27BM2PK.
Even if a sign-off process (GAP-006) existed, it could only catch errors
in content that has been written. The content itself is currently absent.

Both gaps are therefore necessary and non-overlapping.
