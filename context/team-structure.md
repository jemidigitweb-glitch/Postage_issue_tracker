## 2. Team Overview

The Postage Team consists of **4 members** responsible for booking daily orders
across UK, Germany, US, and Canada regions. They create packlists, generate
shipping labels, upload files to Dropbox, and coordinate with packing teams
and couriers.

**Team size:** 4 members
**Regions covered:** UK, Germany, US, Canada
**Shift model:** One morning shift + three overlapping afternoon shifts
**Coordination method:** Dropbox (file handover to warehouse packing teams)

**Source:** Postage_Team_Workflow.pdf §1 Team Overview

## 8. Daily Operational Workflow

The 8-step order journey from receipt to delivery, confirmed from source:

| # | Step | Description |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | Order Received | Daily orders come in from all regions (UK, Germany, US, Canada) |
| 2 | Booking Created | Postage team books orders by region; creates packlists + labels |
| 3 | Upload to Dropbox | Labels and packlists uploaded to shared Dropbox folder |
| 4 | Packing Team Picks | Packing team picks products from warehouse shelves |
| 5 | Pack & Label | Items packed and correct courier label applied |
| 6 | Courier Collection | Courier service visits warehouse, collects parcels |
| 7 | Dispatch to Customer | Courier delivers to end customer |
| 8 | Tracking Update | Person 2 sends courier update emails to team/customers |

For full procedure detail on booking steps (Steps 2–3), see context/bgct-procedures.md.

**Source:** Postage_Team_Workflow.pdf §3 Daily Order Fulfillment Process
