Ownership & Responsibility Gaps 5 issues
Priority Issue What is happening Fix & action required Owner (Sri Lanka)
Critical Manual orders — no single owner Nanthi Akka is repeatedly asked to add picklists for manual orders. Nobody has been assigned to approve, verify payment, pack, and follow up. Every step is unclear, creating constant contact with the UK warehouse. Appoint one named person in SL to own manual orders end-to-end: receive → verify payment (accounts team) → raise picklist → confirm to warehouse → follow up delivery. Document each step. No manual order moves without this person's approval. 👤 Accounts lead + SL coordinator
Critical No backup when key staff are on leave If Mano Ranjini, Varmen, Lakshi, or Sanju are absent, work stops and Nanthi Akka is contacted. There is no documented handover or backup allocation. Information exists only in individual heads. Create a leave-backup matrix: for each role, name who covers which tasks. Before any leave, a mandatory 30-min handover briefing. All active task status stored in a shared tracker. MD to enforce as a non-negotiable standard. 👤 Team leads + MD to enforce
Critical Return parcels — no named responsible person Nyasha example: £225 chandelier with missing parts. No one followed up. DPD did not collect but nobody escalated. Return labels not being booked correctly. Seriousness of customer issues is not being treated properly. Assign a "Returns Owner" for every return case: log it, book the label, confirm collection, and close the loop with the customer within 24–48 hrs. Use a simple returns tracker. Nanthi Akka contacted only if courier refuses — not for first-step actions. 👤 Named customer service lead in SL
High Warehouse supply orders — no cost controller Wrong items delivered to wrong unit (Unit 4 / Unit 3 / Unit 18 address confusion). No one accountable for cost control, correct ordering, and confirming delivery to the right location. One named person owns all warehouse supply orders: raises the PO, confirms delivery address, checks on arrival. Any unit exchange pre-approved and documented. Monthly cost review by accounts team. 👤 Warehouse admin lead
High Amazon / FBA returns — no weekly review meeting Wrong products received in returns (wrong bulb type, wattage — label swapped by Amazon). Pipe lighting returned that is not ours. 350 T185 spiral bulbs returned — root cause was wrong listing (dimmable issue). No weekly meeting to catch patterns early. Hold a 30-min weekly returns review. Compare return slip vs original order. Flag Amazon label-swap cases via Seller Central immediately. Track by SKU — if same product returns repeatedly, escalate listing/barcode audit. Nanthi Akka attends only if pattern unresolved after 2 weeks. 👤 SL e-commerce + accounts team

Listing & Product Issues 4 issues
Priority Issue What is happening Fix & action required Owner (Sri Lanka)
Critical Pick-list images don't match listing images Nanthi Akka has raised this repeatedly — bulb pick-list image and listing image are different. This causes warehouse picking errors and customer complaints. The issue spans general products and bulbs specifically. SL listing team to audit all active listings vs pick-list images this week. Checklist rule: listing image = pick-list image = actual product. Any mismatch corrected before listing stays live. Nanthi Akka approves the final audit result — one interaction, not repeated queries. 👤 SL listing team lead
Critical Cable listing — cut pieces sold as full length 5m cable listing: customers buy 3× thinking they will receive 15m continuous cable, but receive 3 separate cut pieces. The listing does not clearly state this. Causes customer complaints and returns that reach Nanthi Akka. Affects 1m, 5m, and 15m variants. Update all cable listings immediately: clearly state "sold as individual cut lengths — not a continuous full-length cable" in title, bullet points, and description. SL listing team owns this. Review all length variants for the same issue across all cable SKUs. 👤 SL listing team — reviewed by Nanthi Akka once
High Pricing errors — products below profitable price Some products listed below £5 — after postage costs, no profit is made. Price changes are also made without notifying the warehouse team, causing confusion at the packing stage. Morning pricing decisions don't reach evening team. (1) Set a minimum price floor — no product live below cost + postage + margin. Accounts team calculates and maintains this floor per SKU. (2) Any price change must be communicated to warehouse in writing (WhatsApp group or shared doc) before going live. Nanthi Akka should not discover pricing errors after the fact. 👤 SL accounts + listing team
High Stock count inaccurate — orders placed against phantom stock System shows 50 stock but actual count is different. Ceiling rose example: 8 physically in stock, 11 orders placed. Prime orders and first-class orders affected. No physical count reconciliation process in place. Weekly cycle count for fast-moving SKUs. Before any bulk order or prime listing, confirm physical count. Any discrepancy above 10% flagged and investigated same day. SL warehouse admin updates the count — not Nanthi Akka's job to discover this mismatch. 👤 Warehouse team lead

Operational Issues 4 issues
Priority Issue What is happening Fix & action required Owner (Sri Lanka)
Critical Royal Mail size violations Items over 60cm length booked on Royal Mail instead of courier. Pipe lighting must go on Every Courier international pack. Once booked incorrectly, labels cannot be cancelled and parcel returns are uncertain. Nanthi Akka is finding these errors after the fact. Post a size-to-carrier rule card in the packing area: "Over 60cm → Every Courier / International Pack. Pipe lighting → Always Every Courier international." Anyone booking a label checks this before confirming. SL team enforces this — no exceptions. 👤 Warehouse packing lead
High Collection orders — no fixed time or communication process Customers arrive without the warehouse knowing. No standard message is sent to confirm collection is ready or to set a collection window. Nanthi Akka has to intervene to coordinate between the customer and warehouse. (1) Order packed → send customer confirmation with fixed collection window (e.g. "ready 10am–4pm Mon–Fri"). (2) Same forwarded to warehouse WhatsApp group. (3) When collection confirmed ready, second message to customer. SL customer service owns all steps — Nanthi Akka removed from this loop entirely. 👤 SL customer service rep
High Amazon courier missed — no escalation process When Amazon collection is missed, no one sends the follow-up email to the courier. The email is missed, resulting in charges or delays. This is a routine task that repeatedly falls through because no one owns it. If Amazon courier does not collect by cut-off time, designated SL staff sends escalation email within 1 hour using a saved template. Response confirmed in shared tracking doc. This is a routine SL task — not a Nanthi Akka task. 👤 SL dispatch coordinator
Process Slow-moving products — no proactive identification Slow movers are only discovered during picking. No review system exists to flag and action them before they pile up as overstock. 350 T185 spiral bulb return case was partly caused by this — listed as overstock but the real issue was wrong listing detail (dimmable). Monthly slow-mover report from e-commerce/accounts team. Review in weekly meeting. Options: price reduction, bundle, or remove listing. Nanthi Akka receives a summary only — not individual product queries per item. 👤 SL e-commerce lead

Communication & Information Flow 4 issues
Priority Issue What is happening Fix & action required Owner (Sri Lanka)
High Evening team unaware of morning decisions Decisions made in the morning (e.g. pricing changes, first-class order instructions) are not passed to the evening team. Orders get stuck or handled incorrectly because teams operate without shared context. This lack of shift handover causes Nanthi Akka to be contacted to clarify. End-of-morning-shift handover note (5 lines max) sent to a shared WhatsApp group or doc: what changed today, what is pending, what evening team must know. Evening shift lead confirms receipt. Nanthi Akka removed from this loop entirely. 👤 Morning shift lead → Evening shift lead
High Issues broadcast to whole office before being resolved When one issue is found, it is immediately passed across the entire office — causing stop-work on listings and other tasks unnecessarily. This creates panic and confusion rather than controlled resolution. Triage rule: the person who finds an issue owns the first response. They assess: does this need a full stop? If yes, escalate to team lead only — not broadcast. Team lead decides scope. Nanthi Akka contacted only when SL team lead cannot resolve internally. 👤 All SL team leads
Process Public Holiday & Ad-Hoc holiday alerts — no standard system PH and AH dates are not consistently communicated across teams in advance. This causes unplanned gaps in coverage and last-minute contact with Nanthi Akka to cover or advise. All PH and AH dates posted in shared calendar minimum 2 weeks in advance. Auto-reminder to all team leads 3 days before. Nanthi Akka receives a single weekly summary — not individual ad-hoc alerts each time. 👤 HR / admin lead in SL
Process Label inconsistency — 5–6 year ongoing issue Purchasing labels have been inconsistent for years with no enforced standard. Variation in label format causes confusion in the warehouse during picking, packing, and returns processing. SL team creates a label template document with exact format, font, and required fields. All new labels checked against template before printing. One-time audit of existing labels. MD signs off the standard — and enforces it — so it does not keep drifting. 👤 SL warehouse admin + MD approval
